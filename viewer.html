Method (short version)

  • Exophase only refreshes a game's counts every few hours, so the history is first collapsed to its distinct reads — identical consecutive reads carry no information.
  • Each trophy's rate is earners / tracked players. Both sides are fitted in log space against ln(1 + hours since release): ln(tracked players) once, shared by all trophies, and ln(earners) per trophy, its slope shrunk toward the cross-trophy median by empirical Bayes (a precisely measured slope keeps its own value; a noisy one is pulled to the pack).
  • The median is earners ÷ tracked players at the target, blended toward persistence and capped at a 3-logit move. The spread is a prediction interval: a random-walk floor, the fits' prediction errors, and half the trophy's refresh-to-refresh jump volatility.
  • A flat history (no refresh seen yet — the practice cases) shifts the median down 1.5 logits: the next refresh adds many newly tracked players who have earned little yet, so most rates fall. The house's own recorder notes record the first refresh taking tracked players from 638 to 3,868.
